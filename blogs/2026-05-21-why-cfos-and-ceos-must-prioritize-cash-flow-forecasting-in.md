A textile exporter in Tiruppur began 2026 with strong demand from European retailers and steady production schedules. Within months, the Middle East conflict disrupted Red Sea shipping routes, sending freight costs higher and delaying cargo movement. Imported raw material prices fluctuated sharply, shipments took longer to arrive, and customer payments slowed as delivery schedules slipped.
